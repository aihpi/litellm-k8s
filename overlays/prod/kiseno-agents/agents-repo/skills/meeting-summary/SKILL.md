# Meeting summary

1. Read the transcript or notes in the thread.
2. Write the summary in the language of the meeting:
   - one or two sentences on what the meeting was about;
   - decisions, one per line;
   - to-dos with owner and date where they were named.
3. Do not add anything that was not said. Mark unclear owners or dates as "open".
